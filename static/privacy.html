Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026

Auvia Engine ("we", "us") provides audio processing and generation APIs at auviaengine.com. This policy explains what data we collect when you use the site, the API, or the Studio, and what we do with it.

1. What we collect

We collect the minimum needed to run the service:

  • Audio files you upload or record — processed transiently to generate enhanced audio, acoustic intelligence reports, or AI-composed backing tracks.
  • Email address — only if you sign up for API access, request early access, or contact us directly.
  • Basic technical data — standard request logs (IP address, timestamps, endpoint called) generated by the hosting infrastructure, used for abuse prevention and debugging.

We do not require an account or collect payment information to use the free tier or the public demo.

2. What happens to your audio

Audio files are deleted after processing. Uploaded audio is held only for the duration of the request — long enough to run enhancement, analysis, or generation — and is removed from our servers immediately afterward. We do not retain a library of user-uploaded audio, and we do not use your audio to train models.

Some processing steps call third-party AI providers (see Section 4) to do the actual audio work. Those providers receive the audio bytes needed to complete that specific request, subject to their own data-handling terms.

3. No selling of data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data — audio, email, or usage data — to third parties for marketing or any other purpose. Period.

4. Third-party processors

To provide the service, audio and text may be sent to the following processors as part of fulfilling your request:

  • ElevenLabs — text-to-speech and AI music generation
  • Sarvam AI — speech-to-text transcription
  • Google Gemini / Groq — language model reasoning used to describe processing results and generate music prompts
  • Google Cloud Run — hosting infrastructure

Each of these processors has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data in transit through their systems.

5. Your rights (GDPR & general)

If you are located in the EU/EEA or another jurisdiction with equivalent data protection rights, you have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of what personal data we hold about you (in practice, typically limited to an email address if you've contacted us — audio itself is not retained)
  • Request correction or deletion of that data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Since audio is deleted immediately after processing and we don't operate persistent user accounts today, there is generally nothing to export or erase beyond an email address you may have given us — but reach out and we'll act on any request promptly.

6. Cookies & anonymous usage tracking

The free tier uses a locally-stored, anonymous device identifier (no personal information) to apply usage limits. It is not used for tracking across other sites and is not sold or shared.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or a request regarding your data: priyal@auviaengine.com

This policy is written in plain language to be genuinely informative, not just a formality — but it is not a substitute for advice from a qualified privacy lawyer, and you should have one review it before relying on it as a binding legal document for your business.
