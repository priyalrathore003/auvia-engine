Questions

How does the free tier work?

Enhancement and acoustic intelligence are unlimited on every plan. AI backing-track generation (Orchestrate) is capped at 3 uses per device on the free tier, since it's the most expensive endpoint to run — upgrade for unlimited use.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancelling stops future billing immediately; you keep access through the end of the period you already paid for.

What's your refund policy?

Full refund within 7 days of your charge, no questions asked. See Terms of Service for details.

How is payment processed?

Subscriptions are billed via Razorpay. We never see or store your card details.
